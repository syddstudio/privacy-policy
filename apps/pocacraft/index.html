Privacy Policy — Poca Craft

Effective date: 2026-10-10 (first published 2026-09-25)

Poca Craft (the "app") lets you decorate photocards with 3D cream, pearls, gems, glitter, and charms. The app does not collect personal information such as your name, contact details, or location, and your photos and creations stay on your device unless you choose to save or share them. The app is free and shows Google AdMob rewarded ads that you choose to watch (section 2). Starting with version 1.0.1 it also sends anonymous usage statistics that help us improve the app; you can turn them off at any time (section 3).

1. Information the App Handles on Your Device

Photos you choose

When you tap "Choose photo," the app opens your device's system photo picker. The app receives only the photo you select and cannot browse the rest of your library through the picker.

  • The selected photo is used only to display it on your card and is processed entirely on your device.
  • A copy is kept in the app's private storage so you can reopen the card later. It is never uploaded anywhere.

Your cards and settings (stored on device)

Your decorated cards (decoration data, the photo you chose, crop position, and a small preview image), your settings (sound, vibration, language, and usage statistics on/off), how many card slots you have, which material packs you have unlocked, your ad-privacy choices, and the install identifier used for usage statistics (section 3) are stored only in the app's local storage on your device. They are deleted when you delete a card in the app, clear the app's data, or uninstall the app.

Saving and sharing

  • Save saves the finished image to your photo library — on Android in the Pictures/Poca Craft folder, on iPhone in a "Poca Craft" album. The app does not read or upload your other photos.
  • Share opens your device's share sheet. The image goes only to the app or person you choose.

Error log (stored on device)

To help fix problems, the app keeps a short log of recent technical errors (up to 20 entries, no personal content) in local storage. This log itself is never sent automatically (if usage statistics are on, the type and message of errors are sent as described in section 3). If you choose "Send feedback," the recent entries are added to the email draft together with your device model, OS version, app version, language, and install identifier — you can review or delete them before sending.

Store reviews

After you save or share a finished card, the app may ask Google Play or the App Store to show its standard rating prompt. The prompt is shown and handled entirely by the store; the app does not see your rating or review.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows rewarded ads from Google AdMob only when you choose to watch one — for example, to add a card slot or unlock a material pack. To serve and measure these ads, Google and its partners may collect and process data such as your advertising ID (Android advertising ID or Apple IDFA), IP address, and device and ad-interaction information, in accordance with Google's policies.

  • Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland): before any ad is requested, the app shows Google's consent message so you can accept or decline personalized ads. You can change your choice later in the app's info screen under "Ad privacy settings."
  • US state privacy laws: where applicable, you can opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of your personal information for advertising through the same "Ad privacy settings."
  • iPhone (App Tracking Transparency): the app asks for permission before your IDFA can be used. If you decline, ads are still shown but are less personalized, and the app works exactly the same.
  • You can also reset your advertising ID or turn off ad personalization in your device settings (Android: Settings → Google → Ads · iPhone: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking).
  • Ads in the app are limited to content suitable for teens (maximum ad content rating: T).

Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
How Google uses information from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

We do not receive or store any data that identifies you through advertising. We only see aggregate ad reports (for example, how many ads were shown).

3. Usage Statistics (Analytics)

To improve the app — for example, to see where people stop while making a card, which material packs are popular, and which errors occur — the app sends anonymous usage statistics to our analytics service (PostHog). This starts with version 1.0.1; earlier versions send nothing.

What is sentExamples
Usage eventsApp opened/closed (time spent), card-making steps (photo added, first decoration, finished — number of decorations, time taken, whether a photo was used), save/share results, which rewarded-ad spot was watched and whether it was watched to the end or failed to load, material-pack sheet opened/unlocked, settings changes, review prompt requested, feedback opened
App and device infoApp version, platform (Android/iOS), app language, OS version, device type and model, screen size, install source (Android only — the campaign tag attached to the store link, read from the Google Play app on your device)
Error infoType, message, and location of errors that occur in the app
Install identifierOne random value generated by the app. It is not an advertising ID, device serial number, or account, and cannot identify you
  • Not sent: your photos, the contents of your cards, name, email, phone number, contacts, precise location, advertising ID, or IP address (collection disabled).
  • How to turn it off: switch off "Send usage stats" on the info screen and sending stops immediately. In the EEA, UK, and Switzerland it is off by default and is only sent if you turn it on yourself.
  • Storage and international transfer: these statistics are stored as events on servers of our processor, PostHog Inc. (United States). Items: the four listed above. Purpose: analysis to improve the app. When and how: over the internet as the app sends them. Retention: until the app is discontinued or you request deletion. They are not used for advertising, sold, or shared with third parties. See section 9 for deletion.

4. Accounts and Registration

The app has no sign-up or login and never asks for your name, email address, or phone number.

6. Photos of Other People

Please respect the rights of others when you use photos of people. Because your cards stay on your device, we do not host, view, or publish them.

7. Children's Privacy

The app is intended for users aged 13 and older and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, and ads are limited to teen-appropriate content. The usage statistics in section 3 contain nothing that identifies you.

8. Sharing with Third Parties

We do not sell or provide your personal information to third parties. Data processing for ad serving is performed by Google as described in section 2. The usage statistics in section 3 are stored by a processor acting on our behalf (PostHog Inc., United States), which does not use or pass on the data except on our instructions.

9. Your Rights

All app data lives on your device. You can delete individual cards in the app, clear the app's data in your device settings, or uninstall the app to remove everything. Images you saved to your photo library remain there until you delete them. For advertising data, use "Ad privacy settings" in the app or your device's ad settings (section 2). Usage statistics (section 3) can be turned off on the info screen; to request deletion of statistics already sent, email us with the Install id value shown in the email that "Send feedback" opens. The statistics contain nothing that identifies you, so we can only find your records with that value. For other questions about your privacy rights (including under GDPR or US state laws), contact us below.

10. Changes to This Policy

If the app adds features that change how information is handled, this policy will be updated before that version is released.
