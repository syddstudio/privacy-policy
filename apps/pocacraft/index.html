Privacy Policy — Poca Craft

Effective date: 2026-09-25

Poca Craft (the "app") lets you decorate photocards with 3D cream, pearls, gems, glitter, and charms. The app does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information to us or to any server. Your photos and creations stay on your device unless you choose to save or share them yourself.

1. Information the App Handles and Why

Photos you choose

When you tap "Choose photo," the app opens your device's system photo picker. The app can access only the photo you select — it cannot see the rest of your library, and it does not request storage permission.

  • The selected photo is used only to display it on your card and is processed entirely on your device.
  • A copy is kept in the app's private storage so you can reopen the card later. It is never uploaded anywhere.

Your cards (stored on device)

Your decorated cards (decoration data, the photo you chose, crop position, and a small preview image) are stored only in the app's local storage on your device. They are deleted when you delete a card in the app, clear the app's data, or uninstall the app.

Saving and sharing

  • Save to Photos writes the finished image to your device's Pictures/Poca Craft folder. The app does not read other photos in that folder.
  • Share opens your device's share sheet. The image goes only to the app or person you choose.

Error log (stored on device)

To help fix problems, the app keeps a short log of recent technical errors (up to 20 entries, no personal content) in local storage. It is never sent automatically. If you choose "Send feedback," the recent entries are added to the email draft together with your device model, OS version, app version, and language — you can review or delete them before sending.

2. Accounts and Registration

The app has no sign-up or login and never asks for your name, email address, or phone number.

3. Permissions

The app does not request access to your photo library, camera, contacts, or location. Photos are provided through the system photo picker only when you choose them.

4. Photos of Other People

Please respect the rights of others when you use photos of people. Because your cards stay on your device, we do not host, view, or publish them.

5. Children's Privacy

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children, because it does not collect personal information at all.

6. Sharing with Third Parties

We do not sell or provide your information to third parties. The app contains no advertising or analytics SDKs.

7. Your Rights

All app data lives on your device. You can delete individual cards in the app, clear the app's data in Android settings, or uninstall the app to remove everything. Images you saved to Pictures/Poca Craft remain in your gallery until you delete them.

8. Changes to This Policy

If the app adds features that change how information is handled (for example, in-app purchases through Google Play), this policy will be updated before that version is released.
