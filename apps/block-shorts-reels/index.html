Privacy Policy — Block Shorts & Reels

Effective date: August 2, 2026

Block Shorts & Reels (the "app") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information the app handles and how. In short, the app does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information that identifies you. Everything needed to block short-form video screens is processed in real time, entirely on your device.

1. Information the App Accesses and Why

Screen detection (Accessibility Service)

The app uses the Android Accessibility Service to detect short-form video screens such as YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels, TikTok, Naver Clip, and KakaoTalk short-form feeds. To do this, it reads which app is currently on screen and the screen's UI structure.

  • This information is used only on your device, in real time, to decide whether the current screen should be blocked.
  • It is never stored, logged, or sent to any external server. It is discarded immediately after the decision is made.
  • The app does not collect anything you type, your passwords, messages, or personal content.

List of installed apps (whole-app blocking feature)

The "block entire app" feature blocks apps you choose yourself. To show you candidates on the selection screen, the app reads the names and icons of installed apps that have a launcher icon on your home screen.

  • This list is used only to render the selection screen and is never stored or transmitted.
  • Only the package names of apps you explicitly choose to block are saved as settings on your device.
  • The app does not use the QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES permission. Instead of querying every installed app, it narrows the query to apps with a launcher icon — the minimum needed.

App settings (stored on device)

Settings such as per-platform and per-app blocking on/off, your chosen block list, today's block count, and the "allow for 5 minutes" expiry time are stored only in your device's local storage and are never transmitted. They are deleted when you uninstall the app.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows Google AdMob rewarded ads for features such as the "allow for 5 minutes" unlock. To serve ads, Google and its partners may collect and process data such as your advertising ID, IP address, and device/usage information, in accordance with Google's policies.

  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • About Google ads: https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543

The app developer does not separately collect any personal information through advertising.

2. Accounts and Registration

The app has no sign-up or login, and never asks for personal information such as your name, email address, or phone number.

4. Children's Privacy

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

5. Sharing with Third Parties

The app developer does not sell or provide your personal information to third parties. Data processing related to ad serving is governed by the Google AdMob section above.

6. Your Rights

Because the app does not collect or store personal information on any server, there is no server-side data to access, correct, or delete — all app data lives on your device and is removed when you uninstall the app. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalization at any time in your device's Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads). If you have questions about your privacy rights (including rights under GDPR or CCPA), contact us at the address below.
