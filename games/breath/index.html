Privacy Policy — Breath

Effective date: DRAFT (to be set at release)

Breath (the "game") is a free single-player game supported by optional rewarded ads, occasional interstitial ads, and in-app purchases. The game has no accounts and no analytics, and the developer does not collect or store personal information on any server. The only third party that processes data is Google AdMob, for showing ads, and Google Play, for purchases. This page explains exactly what that means.

1. Information Stored on Your Device

DataPurpose
Save data (coins, upgrades, gear, best records, codex)Keep your progress between sessions
Purchase flags (ad removal, unlocked wrecks)Apply what you bought
SettingsSound and language preferences
  • This data describes game progress only. It contains no name, email, phone number, location, contacts, or photos.
  • It stays on your device. Uninstalling the game removes all of it.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows two kinds of ads through Google AdMob: rewarded ads you choose to watch for an in-game bonus, and interstitial ads shown only between runs (never during play, and never during your first runs). To serve and measure ads, the AdMob SDK may process:

  • Your device's advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID), which you can reset or limit in your device settings.
  • Approximate location derived from IP address, device type and OS version, and ad interaction data (impressions, clicks).

This processing is governed by Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. Where required (for example in the EEA and UK), the game shows Google's consent form before any ad is loaded, and you can choose non-personalized ads. Purchasing "Remove Ads" stops interstitial ads; rewarded ads remain available only if you tap them.

3. Purchases (Google Play Billing)

All in-app products are fixed-price items with no random rewards. Payment is handled entirely by Google Play: the developer never sees your card number or billing address. The game receives only a purchase token to unlock what you bought. Refunds follow Google Play's policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

4. What the Game Does Not Do

  • No accounts. No sign-up, login, or profile.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting SDK beyond what the AdMob SDK itself reports to Google.
  • No cross-device sync. Saves exist only on the device that created them.

5. Permissions

PlatformPermissions
AndroidINTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE (required by the ad and billing SDKs), com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID (advertising identifier for AdMob). No camera, microphone, location, contacts, or storage permissions.

6. Children's Privacy

The game is not directed at children under 13. If you are a parent and believe a child has used the game and ads were personalized, reset the device's advertising identifier and contact us below.

7. Sharing with Third Parties

The developer does not sell or share personal information. The only third parties are Google AdMob (advertising) and Google Play (payments), each acting under its own privacy policy as described above.

8. Your Rights

The developer holds no server-side record of you. To remove the game's local data, uninstall the game. To control ad personalization, use your device's ad settings or the in-app consent options where shown. EEA/UK residents may lodge a complaint with their local data protection authority; California residents: we do not sell or share personal information as defined by the CCPA/CPRA.

9. Changes to This Policy

Material changes will be announced on this page and recorded in the revision history below.
