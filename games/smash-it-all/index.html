Privacy Policy — Smash It All

Effective date: 2026-09-24

Smash It All (the "game") is a free single-player game supported only by optional rewarded ads. The game has no accounts, no in-app purchases and no analytics, and the developer does not collect or store personal information on any server. The only third party that processes data is Google AdMob, for showing the ads you choose to watch. This page explains exactly what that means.

1. Information Stored on Your Device

DataPurpose
Save data (current run, cash, tools, career profile and records)Keep your progress between sessions
SettingsControls, display, sound and language preferences
Error log (last few errors, on the device only)Kept on the device only, for troubleshooting; never sent automatically
  • This data describes game progress only. It contains no name, email, phone number, location, contacts, or photos.
  • It stays on your device. Uninstalling the game removes all of it.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows rewarded ads only when you choose to watch one (for a payout bonus after a floor). There are no banner or interstitial ads. To serve and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may process:

  • Your device's advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID or Apple IDFA), which you can reset or limit in your device settings.
  • Other device identifiers (such as the app set ID), IP address and the approximate location derived from it, device type and OS version.
  • Ad interaction data (impressions, taps, video views) and diagnostics (such as launch time and crash rate) used for fraud prevention and measurement.

This processing is governed by Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. Where required (for example in the EEA and UK), the game shows Google's consent form before any ad is loaded, and you can choose non-personalized ads. On iOS, the game asks for tracking permission (App Tracking Transparency) before the advertising identifier can be used.

3. What the Game Does Not Do

  • No accounts. No sign-up, login, or profile on any server.
  • No in-app purchases.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting SDK beyond what the AdMob SDK itself reports to Google.
  • No cross-device sync. Saves exist only on the device that created them.

4. Permissions

5. Children's Privacy

The game is not directed at children under 13. If you are a parent and believe a child has used the game and ads were personalized, reset the device's advertising identifier and contact us below.

6. Sharing with Third Parties

The developer does not sell or share personal information. The only third party is Google AdMob (advertising), acting under its own privacy policy as described above.

7. Your Rights

The developer holds no server-side record of you. To remove the game's local data, uninstall the game. To control ad personalization, use your device's ad settings or the consent options shown in the game where required. EEA/UK residents may lodge a complaint with their local data protection authority; California residents: we do not sell or share personal information as defined by the CCPA/CPRA.

8. Changes to This Policy

Material changes will be announced on this page and recorded in the revision history below.

9. Contact

Privacy inquiries: support@syddstudio.com
