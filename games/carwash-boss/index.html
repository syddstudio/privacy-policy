Privacy Policy — Car Wash Boss

Effective date: 2026-10-18

Car Wash Boss (Korean title 세차장 사장님, "the Game") is a free single-player game supported by optional rewarded ads and interstitial ads between cars. It has no accounts and no in-app purchases, and it does not ask for personal information such as your name or contact details. Data is processed in two places: Google AdMob, for ads, and anonymous play statistics (PostHog) used to improve the game, which you can turn off in settings. This page explains exactly what that means.

1. Information stored on your device

InformationPurpose
Save data (money, upgrades, level, decorations, branches, car book, achievements, missions, daily rewards)To continue your progress next time
Settings (language, sound, vibration, screen effects, accessibility, graphics, play statistics on/off)To keep the settings you chose
Install identifier (section 3)To group play statistics by install
  • This is only game progress and settings (plus a random install identifier). It contains no name, email, phone number, location, contacts or photos.
  • It stays on your device (app storage), and deleting the Game deletes all of it.

2. Ads (Google AdMob)

The Game shows two kinds of ads through Google AdMob: rewarded ads you choose to watch to double or triple a reward or get free in-game cash, and interstitial ads that appear only after you finish a wash and tap "Next car" (never while you are washing, not in the first few minutes after you start, and not within a few minutes of the previous ad). To serve and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may process:

  • Your device's advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID or Apple IDFA), which you can reset or limit in your device settings.
  • Other device identifiers (such as the app set ID), your IP address and the approximate location derived from it, and your device type and OS version.
  • Ad interaction data (impressions, taps, video views) and diagnostic data for fraud prevention and measurement (such as launch time and crash rate).

This processing follows the Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. Where the law requires it (such as the EEA and the UK), the Game shows Google's consent form before loading ads, and you can change your choice later under "Ad privacy settings" in the Game's settings. On iPhone, the first time an ad is shown, Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt asks whether you allow tracking. If you don't, the Game and its ads work the same, with non-personalized ads.

3. Play statistics (analytics)

To fix and improve the game — where players stop, how long they play, which errors happen — the Game sends anonymous play statistics to the developer's analytics service (PostHog).

What is sentExamples
Play eventsApp open and close (time played), starting or continuing the game, finishing the first wash (time taken), level-ups (level, branch, play time), opening a new branch, where an ad was watched and its type, settings changes, review prompt requests and opening feedback
App and device informationApp version, platform (Android/iOS), app language, OS version, device type, screen size
Error informationType, message and location of errors inside the Game
Install identifierOne random value generated by the Game. It is not an advertising ID, device serial number or account, and cannot identify you
  • Not sent: name, email, phone number, contacts, precise location, advertising ID, IP address (configured not to be collected).
  • How to turn it off: turn off "Send play stats" in settings and sending stops immediately. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland it is off by default and is only sent if you turn it on yourself.
  • Storage and international transfer: these statistics are stored as events on servers of PostHog Inc. (USA), which processes them on the developer's behalf. Purpose: analysis to improve the Game. They are sent over the internet as they happen and kept until the Game's service ends or you ask for deletion. They are not used for advertising, sold or shared. See "Your rights" for deletion.

4. Ratings

The Game may ask you to rate it using Google Play In-App Review or the Apple App Store rating prompt. Ratings and reviews go directly to the store; the Game cannot see them.

5. Sending feedback

Tapping "Feedback" in settings opens your device's mail app with your device model, OS version, game version and language (and the install identifier, if play statistics are on) pre-filled to help solve problems. It reaches the developer only if you send the email yourself, and you can delete or edit it before sending. Emails are used only to reply and fix problems.

6. What the Game does not do

  • No accounts. No sign-up, sign-in or server profile.
  • No in-app purchases. Money in the Game is play currency only and cannot be bought with real money.
  • No cross-device sync. Save data stays on the device that created it.

7. Permissions

PlatformPermissions
AndroidINTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE (required by the ads SDK), com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID (AdMob advertising identifier), VIBRATE (game vibration — can be turned off in settings). No camera, microphone, location, contacts or storage permissions.
iOSApp Tracking Transparency (ATT) — asked once, the first time an ad is shown. No other permissions are requested.

8. Children's privacy

The Game is not directed to children under 13. If you are a parent and believe your child used the Game and was shown personalized ads, please reset the device's advertising identifier and contact us below.

9. Sharing with third parties

The developer does not sell or share personal information. Google AdMob processes data to serve ads, and Google Play / Apple App Store handle ratings, each under its own privacy policy. Play statistics are stored by a service that processes data on the developer's behalf (PostHog Inc., USA) and does not use or pass on the data without the developer's instructions.

10. Your rights

Game progress stays on your device and is deleted when you delete the Game. Play statistics can be turned off in settings. To request deletion of statistics already sent, email us at the address below with the Install id value shown in the email opened by "Feedback" in settings (the statistics contain nothing that identifies you, so we need that value to find the records). You can adjust ad personalization in your device's ad settings (on iPhone: Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking), or, where shown, in the Game's "Ad privacy settings".
