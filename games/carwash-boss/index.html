Privacy Policy — Car Wash Boss

Effective date: 2026-10-18

Car Wash Boss (Korean title 세차장 사장님, "the Game") is a free single-player game supported by optional rewarded ads and interstitial ads between cars. It has no accounts, no analytics tools and no in-app purchases, and the developer does not collect or store personal information on any server. The only third party that processes data is Google AdMob, for ads. This page explains exactly what that means.

1. Information stored on your device

InformationPurpose
Save data (money, upgrades, level, decorations, branches, car book, achievements, missions, daily rewards)To continue your progress next time
Settings (language, sound, vibration, screen effects, accessibility, graphics)To keep the settings you chose
  • This is only game progress and settings. It contains no name, email, phone number, location, contacts or photos.
  • It stays on your device (app storage), and deleting the Game deletes all of it.

2. Ads (Google AdMob)

The Game shows two kinds of ads through Google AdMob: rewarded ads you choose to watch to double or triple a reward or get free in-game cash, and interstitial ads that appear only after you finish a wash and tap "Next car" (never while you are washing, not in the first few minutes after you start, and not within a few minutes of the previous ad). To serve and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may process:

  • Your device's advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID or Apple IDFA), which you can reset or limit in your device settings.
  • Other device identifiers (such as the app set ID), your IP address and the approximate location derived from it, and your device type and OS version.
  • Ad interaction data (impressions, taps, video views) and diagnostic data for fraud prevention and measurement (such as launch time and crash rate).

This processing follows the Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. Where the law requires it (such as the EEA and the UK), the Game shows Google's consent form before loading ads, and you can change your choice later under "Ad privacy settings" in the Game's settings. On iPhone, the first time an ad is shown, Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt asks whether you allow tracking. If you don't, the Game and its ads work the same, with non-personalized ads.

3. Ratings

The Game may ask you to rate it using Google Play In-App Review or the Apple App Store rating prompt. Ratings and reviews go directly to the store; the Game cannot see them.

4. Sending feedback

Tapping "Feedback" in settings opens your device's mail app with your device model, OS version, game version and language pre-filled to help solve problems. It reaches the developer only if you send the email yourself, and you can delete or edit it before sending. Emails are used only to reply and fix problems.

5. What the Game does not do

  • No accounts. No sign-up, sign-in or server profile.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting SDKs, other than what the AdMob SDK reports to Google.
  • No in-app purchases. Money in the Game is play currency only and cannot be bought with real money.
  • No cross-device sync. Save data stays on the device that created it.

6. Permissions

PlatformPermissions
AndroidINTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE (required by the ads SDK), com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID (AdMob advertising identifier), VIBRATE (game vibration — can be turned off in settings). No camera, microphone, location, contacts or storage permissions.
iOSApp Tracking Transparency (ATT) — asked once, the first time an ad is shown. No other permissions are requested.

7. Children's privacy

The Game is not directed to children under 13. If you are a parent and believe your child used the Game and was shown personalized ads, please reset the device's advertising identifier and contact us below.

8. Sharing with third parties

The developer does not sell or share personal information. The only third parties are Google AdMob (ads) and Google Play / Apple App Store (ratings), as described above, each under its own privacy policy.

9. Your rights

The developer holds no records about you on any server. To delete the Game's on-device data, delete the Game. You can adjust ad personalization in your device's ad settings (on iPhone: Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking), or, where shown, in the Game's "Ad privacy settings".

10. Changes to this policy

Significant changes will be posted on this page and recorded in the revision history below.
