Privacy Policy — Three Realms

Effective date: August 28, 2026

Three Realms (삼국천하, the "game") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information the game handles and how. In short, the game does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. It is a single-player offline game: it has no accounts, no advertising, and no analytics, and it does not request internet access at all. Everything you do in the game stays on your device.

1. Information Stored on Your Device

The game saves the following to your device's local storage so that your progress survives between sessions. None of it is transmitted anywhere.

DataPurpose
Saved games (multiple slots)Resume a campaign in progress
Clear recordsTrack which rulers you have completed the game with
Encyclopedia progressUnlock officer biographies and treasure entries you have encountered
Custom ruler dataStore a ruler you created yourself
SettingsSound on/off, music and effect volume, language
  • This data describes game progress only. It contains no name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, or any other information that identifies you.
  • On mobile the same data is mirrored to the operating system's app preference storage (Android SharedPreferences / iOS UserDefaults) so that a save is not lost if the web view's storage is cleared. This is still local storage on your device.
  • You can erase it at any time from the game's settings screen, and uninstalling the game removes all of it.

2. What the Game Does Not Do

  • No accounts. There is no sign-up, no login, and no user profile.
  • No advertising. The game contains no ad SDK and no advertising identifier is read.
  • No analytics or tracking. There is no analytics SDK, no crash reporting SDK, and no third-party tracker of any kind.
  • No internet access. The Android build declares no permissions at all, including INTERNET. All artwork, music, and sound effects are bundled inside the app and loaded from local files.
  • No cross-device sync. Saves exist only on the device that created them.

3. Permissions

PlatformPermissions requested
AndroidNone. The app manifest declares no uses-permission entries.
iOSNone. The app declares no privacy usage descriptions (no camera, microphone, location, contacts, photos, or tracking).

The game uses your device's vibration feature for haptic feedback, which does not require a permission or access any data.

4. External Links

The settings screen can open the app's store page (Google Play or the App Store) so you can leave a review. Selecting it hands the address to the store app or your browser and leaves the game. Those services have their own privacy policies, and we receive nothing from them beyond the aggregate sales and review information the stores show every developer.

5. Purchases

The game is sold as a one-time paid purchase. Payment is handled entirely by Google Play or the Apple App Store — the purchase never happens inside the game, and the developer never sees or receives your card number, billing address, or any other payment credential. Refunds and payment records are governed by the store's own policy.

  • Google Play: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Apple: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

6. Children's Privacy

The game is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. Because no information is collected, no parental consent mechanism is required.

7. Sharing with Third Parties

We do not sell, share, or transfer personal information to third parties. There is no third-party SDK embedded in the game, so no data is disclosed to anyone by the game's operation.

8. Your Rights

Because the game stores nothing on any server, there is no server-side record of you to access, correct, export, or delete. All game data lives on your device: you can erase it from the settings screen or by uninstalling the game.

If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, you have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority. If you are a California resident, note that we do not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined by the CCPA/CPRA. Any questions about your privacy rights can be sent to the address below.

9. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be revised when necessary. Material changes will be announced on this page, and the revision history below records every version.

10. Contact

Privacy inquiries: support@syddstudio.com
